## 1. the eval bug

while verifying the new teacher, our pipeline measured 73% on math-500 against the model card's 92.8%. way outside any reasonable noise band, so we dug.

two things were broken at once.

(i) sympy's `parse_latex` needs `antlr4-python3-runtime==4.11`. our venv had `4.13.2+computecanada` (compute canada's bundle), which is api-incompatible. every `parse_latex` call raised `ImportError`. `math_verify` silently catches it and returns `False`. no warnings, no errors, just every latex-bearing answer marked wrong.

(ii) even with antlr4 fixed, `math_verify` lacks the pre-normalization that deepseek's eval has: `\dfrac` vs `\frac`, `\text{evelyn}` vs `evelyn`, `\left(...\right)` vs `(...)`, whitespace differences — all marked wrong even when the answers are equivalent.

| comparator | r1 math-500 acc |
|---|---|
| our pipeline (`math_verify`, broken antlr4) | 73.00% |
| `math_verify` alone after antlr4 fix | 90.20% |
| **deepseek's full pipeline** (`strip_string` + `math_equal` + multi-candidate) | **93.00%** |

how it stayed hidden: no exception ever surfaced, the broken numbers looked plausible for a 0.5b student on math, and the bug hit all methods symmetrically so relative differences between methods were preserved within a few pp. we only noticed because the new teacher was strong enough that the gap was too big to wave away.

ported deepseek-math's `math_equal`, `strip_string`, and `extract_math_answer` into `shared/math_eval.py`. wired it through reward computation (`dg-offline/teacher_agnostic_loader.py`), student eval (`mixture_grpo/evaluate.py`), and teacher verification. full diagnosis lives in `docs/eval_methodology.md`.

reward density on existing qwen-math rollouts under the upgraded comparator: **80.9%** correct on shard 0 of math train (was lower under the broken comparator). that's a chunk of training signal we silently lost across every prior run.

spot-checked the old teacher itself on math-500 under the upgraded comparator: **83.80%** greedy vs the **74.96%** we previously reported. **+8.84pp** purely from the comparator fix, same model, same prompt, same greedy decoding. confirms the bug isn't theoretical, and means r1's lead over qwen-math is closer to 9pp than the 18pp we'd estimated yesterday.

## 2. multi-teacher experiment

last week's surprisal diagnostic showed dg's gate fires on only 1-6% of teacher rollouts in our same-family setup. hypothesis: a stronger and stylistically distinct teacher should help dg specifically. two mechanisms: (i) higher correct rate → more groups with non-zero advantage → less collapse, (ii) correct rollouts that surprise the student → dg's "amplify surprising successes" branch finally fires.

teacher: deepseek-r1-distill-qwen-7b. claimed ~93% math-500 (verified). reasoning-style traces, stylistically distant from qwen-math's terser proofs. tokenizer family matches our existing teacher (base model is qwen2.5-math-7b).

6 configs locked: bc-all, bc-correct-only, dg-offline at η ∈ {0.1, 0.5, 1.0, 2.0}. offline-grpo on r1 reported as **n/a** — r1's `<think>` and `</think>` token ids collide with unrelated qwen-base special tokens in the student's vocab, breaking the legacy is-ratio loader's alignment. supporting og-on-r1 properly needs ~1-2 gpu-hrs of new infra (computing teacher logprobs against the student tokenization). deferred.

dataset: just math for the first cut. audit: cheap 5-seed eval on all 6 → full audit only on the per-method winners.

full design in `docs/dg_offline/plans/multi_teacher_experiment.md`.

## 3. teacher-agnostic loader

dg-offline as a method only needs (text, reward) from the teacher — tokenizer compatibility is irrelevant in principle. but our prior code didn't actually exercise this: the trainer reused `offline_grpo/data.py`'s loader, which carries pre-tokenized teacher token ids straight to the student forward pass. silent corruption mode if teacher and student vocabs differ at any id.

built `dg-offline/teacher_agnostic_loader.py` from scratch. self-contained (no imports from `offline_grpo/`), reads response text, re-tokenizes under the student tokenizer. switched `dg-offline/train.py` to use it. smoke-tested end to end: reward and advantage distributions match the legacy loader exactly on existing rollouts, and a 10-step training run on 4 l40s completed clean.
