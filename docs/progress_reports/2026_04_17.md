## 1. Variance Audit: Why and How

### Why

`results_snapshot_2026_04_12.md` records a known phenomenon: the MATH baseline measured at 27.16% on one eval job and 30.36% on another, same script, same seeds, different GPU nodes. The cause is vLLM fp16 kernel non-determinism across hardware. The practical effect: a single 5-seed eval job can land anywhere in a roughly ±3pp band around the true value. This is comparable in size to most of our between-method gaps on GSM8K (0.5-1.5pp) and between η values (0.3-1.2pp), so **single-run eval numbers cannot distinguish method A from method B**, only confidence intervals can.

Until now every table cell came from one 5-seed single-job eval. Good enough for internal tracking, not good enough to publish.

### How (three orthogonal sources of variance)

Three noise sources interact. Our audit design separates them so we know which ones matter.

| Source | What it is | Captured by |
|---|---|---|
| **Seed noise within a job** | sampling/tie-breaking drift across different `SamplingParams(seed=...)` calls on one loaded model | running many seeds inside one SLURM job |
| **Job-to-job vLLM noise on the same node** | GPU state, KV-cache allocation, concurrent workload | submitting the same eval as a fresh SLURM job (rarely same node anyway) |
| **Cross-node bf16 kernel noise** | different GPU hardware chooses different matmul kernels | submitting multiple independent SLURM jobs, which land on different racks |

The first is cheap to measure (one 30-seed job, std typically <0.5pp). The third is the big one (up to 3pp). Job 2 and job 3 collapse in practice because every eval submission ends up on a different node on Vulcan.

### Audit protocols used this period

| Protocol | Used on | What it gives |
|---|---|---|
| **Greedy audit** = 2 independent SLURM jobs × 30 seeds each (60 seeds pooled) at temp=0.0 | GSM8K: BC-all, BC-cc, DG-η=0.1, DG-η=0.5, DG-η=1.0 | Within-job std + between-job spread on greedy accuracy |
| **pass@N audit** = 3 independent SLURM jobs × seeds {42, 43, 44}, each does best-of-16 sampling at temp=0.6 | GSM8K: BC-cc, DG-η=0.1, DG-η=1.0 | Mean ± std on pass@1 and pass@16 |
| **Greedy 30-seed single-job** | SVAMP: all 7 rows | Tight within-job bound; does not isolate cross-node noise but SVAMP's spread made that sufficient |

Implementation: `shared/run_eval.sh` was extended with a `SEED` env var so each SLURM submission could use a different sampling seed (previously hardcoded to 42). Jobs are submitted with distinct `--job-name` so they end up on different schedulable nodes.

## 3. Generation-Cap Bug on ASDiv

ASDiv and SVAMP were registered with `max_tokens=512, max_model_len=1024`, sized for the teacher's concise output distribution. The untrained 0.5B reasons verbosely and does not use `\boxed{}` by default, so at 512 tokens its completions were being sliced mid-computation; the extractor's last-number fallback then grabbed an intermediate arithmetic step.

Diagnostic run at `max_tokens=1024` on all 461 ASDiv test problems (job 4717004): baseline **jumps from 39.09% to 72.9%**. Every wrong example at 512 tokens ended mid-sentence without emitting `\boxed{}`.

Fix: bumped both datasets to `max_tokens=1024, max_model_len=2048` in `shared/datasets_registry.py`, then re-ran every cell in the SVAMP and ASDiv tables (jobs 4717023-36). Trained methods moved ≤3pp (most <1pp) because they already learned the concise teacher-style output. Only the ASDiv baseline moved materially.

| | SVAMP baseline Δ | ASDiv baseline Δ | Any trained method Δ |
|---|---|---|---|
| Greedy after fix | +0.27pp | **+34.49pp** | ≤ +3.13pp |

Impact: the "BC +34pp on ASDiv" row was an eval artifact, not a training result. After the fix, BC sits within 1pp of the corrected baseline on ASDiv, consistent with its behavior on MATH and GSM8K.
