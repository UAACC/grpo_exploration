## 1. dg-madz sweep on gsm8k

motivation. dg's gate is `σ(η · χ)` with `χ = advantage · surprisal`. ran a diagnostic on the empirical χ distribution across our 4 teacher datasets. two findings:

(i) 85-94% of tokens sit at χ=0. their rollout group has zero reward variance, so advantage is zero, and the gate has no signal to act on.

(ii) the remaining 6-15% "active" subset is heavy-tailed and negatively skewed. std/mad ratio is 11-22× across the 4 datasets. dispersion is dominated by a thin tail of outliers, not the typical case.

(iii) is the load-bearing one. under raw `σ(χ/η)`, 88-99% of active samples land in the sigmoid's linear regime around 0.5. the gate's four-quadrant filter (the whole point of dg) wasn't firing. on the active subset, the gate had degenerated into a flat 0.5× multiplier.

fix: replace `χ` with mad-z `z = (χ - median) / (1.4826 · mad)`. now the typical active sample has |z| ~ 1, and `σ(z/η)` at η near 1 actually saturates. the gate can do the quadrant filtering it was designed for.

trained 4 etas. η=5.0 is new; the prior dg-offline sweep stopped at 2.0.

| method | n | greedy ± std | pass@1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| dg-madz η=0.5 | 60 | 49.34 ± 0.10 | 49.66% |
| **dg-madz η=1.0** | 60 | **49.85 ± 0.29** | 47.01% |
| dg-madz η=2.0 | 60 | 48.70 ± 0.12 | 45.79% |
| dg-madz η=5.0 | 60 | 48.70 ± 0.22 | 45.26% |

best is η=1.0 at 49.85%, tied with bc-cc (50.21%) and the prior dg-offline best (η=0.1 at 50.16%; see section 2). fixing the gate didn't move the headline number.

interpretation: gate firing wasn't the bottleneck on gsm8k. it's (i). with 85-94% of tokens at advantage=0, the gate can only reweight the small active subset, and whether it fires correctly on that subset doesn't compensate for the deadweight. mad-z fixes the gate, not the signal-density problem.

### what changes vs prior reports

gsm8k is much less sensitive to the comparator fix than math. teacher 7b at 95.68% sits within 0.06pp of the prior 95.74%. baseline at 47.92% sits within 0.24pp of the prior 48.16%. trained rows shifted between −0.7 and +0.8pp. mechanism: gsm8k extracts the answer as a number from "#### N" format, not latex, so the antlr4-induced parse_latex no-op almost never hit the gsm8k critical path. the math 5-15pp undercount was latex-specific.

three rows didn't fit the small-shift pattern. both are 5pp+ drops, far outside the comparator noise band. investigated both.

two-stage: my audit merged the stage-2 lora into the wrong base. used qwen-0.5b-instruct, should have used `merged/dg_offline_gsm8k_eta0.5` (the base the stage-2 lora was trained against). re-audit with correct base: 48.59% ± 0.32 greedy. recovers most of the apparent drop. the residual 2pp gap to the prior 50.61 is from eval-pipeline drift (older script's prompt format), not calibration. same wrong-base bug on math but didn't bite (corrected math two-stage = 37.75 vs the buggy 37.56).

online-cross-task: traced the prior 55.82% to `online-grpo-eval-4299873` (march 10, pre-shuai14→mrli migration). that model was `online_grpo_gsm8k_merged_step7000`, trained on gsm8k, eval'd on gsm8k. on-task, not cross-task. the report labeled it "math ckpt, cross-task" but the actual model was a separate on-task gsm8k run whose checkpoint was wiped in the path migration. my 50.32% is the genuine cross-task number for `online_grpo_math_merged`. old reports conflated two unrelated rows. no anomaly, just a labeling mistake.

## 3. awbc and rwbc

continued the gate question from section 1. if dg's gate had degenerated under raw scaling, what was it actually contributing? stripped it out and tested.

**awbc** (advantage-weighted bc). loss = `-A · log π_student`. keeps the group-normalized advantage, drops the is ratio, ppo clip, and sigmoid gate. minimal advantage-aware method. trained on all 4 qwen-math era datasets, audited under the lean protocol.

| dataset | awbc greedy ± std | comparison cluster |
|---|---|---|
| math | 32.12 ± 0.45 | bc-cc 31.13, dg-best 33.21 (both calib) |
| gsm8k | 50.21 ± 0.44 | bc-cc 50.21, dg-best 50.16 (both calib, exact tie) |
| svamp | 64.01 ± 0.15 | bc 65.67, dg-best 65.93 (pre-calib, ~1pp diff likely calibration) |
| asdiv | 74.88 ± 0.74 | bc 74.36, dg-best 76.23 (pre-calib) |

awbc lands inside the bc/dg cluster on all 4 datasets. so dg's gate isn't earning its keep (consistent with section 1's diagnostic), and og's is + ppo clip aren't earning theirs either. the modest gain over baseline that bc/dg/og/awbc share is whatever the advantage signal pulls out of the small active subset. everything else is window dressing.

**rwbc considered, abandoned.** proposed: loss = `-r · log π_student` (vanilla reinforce on raw reward, no advantage, no gate, no is). caught in pre-flight that for binary r ∈ {0, 2.0}, the loss reduces to bc-correct-only with lr scaled by 2.0: wrong rollouts contribute zero, correct rollouts contribute uniformly. would just rederive bc-cc numbers. didn't train.
