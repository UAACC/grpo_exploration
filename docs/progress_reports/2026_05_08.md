## 1. pipeline fix

fix:
- `bc/data.py`: re-tokenize the response text under the student tokenizer at load time, throw away the stored `completion_ids`.
- `offline_grpo/data.py`: keep using the stored ids, but assert that a sample of them round-trips cleanly under the student tokenizer (decoded text equals or is a prefix of the original response text). fail loud at load time if not.
- `offline_grpo/train.py`: load the student tokenizer before `load_rollouts` and pass it in for the safety check.
- `mixture_grpo/data.py`: same check, plus a deprecated docstring.

the round-trip check is dynamic, doesn't hardcode any vocab size or special-token id, so it stays correct under tokenizer changes. smoke tested: qwen-math rollouts pass; r1-distill raw rollouts fail 199/200 sampled records as expected.

## 2. long-context training infra

bc on r1-distill rollouts needed a 16k context window. three memory walls hit, three fixes:

(i) bc training oom at 16k. vanilla hf cross-entropy materializes a `[seq, vocab]` fp32 log_softmax at every step (~10 gib at 16k × 152k vocab). patched the model with liger kernel's fused linear cross-entropy (`apply_liger_kernel_to_qwen2()`); fused-linear-ce streams the lm_head matmul + softmax + nll inline over vocab chunks, so neither logits nor log_softmax exist as full tensors. v4-liger trained cleanly to convergence.

(ii) og-prep oom in vllm `prompt_logprobs`. five consecutive failures, last in `vllm/v1/sample/sampler.py:compute_logprobs`. switched to a transformers-direct forward pass with `gather + chunked-fp32 logsumexp`, which never materializes the full distribution. ran end-to-end on 12k items × 4 generations × max 38k context, peak gpu 27.6 gib, 0 drops.

(iii) og-on-r1d trainer oom. trl's grpo trainer plus accelerate's `convert_to_fp32` wrapper was casting student + reference logits to fp32 (~37 gib at 8k seq × 152k vocab × num_gen=4). monkey-patched `convert_to_fp32` to no-op (model is already bf16) and dropped `num_generations` 4 → 2. first epoch in 7h07m, no further oom.

### what's actually happening

ran a 50-problem diagnostic on all 6 r1-distill students with completions saved. headline observations:

| model | median completion length | %hit max_tokens | %\boxed{} present |
|---|---|---|---|
| bc-all | 16384 | 78% | 22% |
| bc-cc | 16384 | 76% | 28% |
| dg η=0.1 | 16384 | 94% | 8% |
| dg η=0.5 | 16384 | 96% | 4% |
| dg η=1.0 | 16384 | 76% | 24% |
| **dg η=2.0** | **598** | **12%** | **88%** |

bc and low/mid-η dg learn to imitate r1-distill's verbose chain-of-thought, then run out of token budget mid-reasoning, never reach `\boxed{}`. dg η=2.0 has effectively learned *not* to imitate the verbose cot. it produces 600-token textbook-style solutions and reaches `\boxed{}` 88% of the time. og-on-r1d behaves similarly (~3000-token completions, mostly terminating).

side-by-side on a representative problem ("how many positive whole-number divisors does 196 have?", gold = 9):
- **bc-all** (16384 tokens, wrong): brute-force trial division "196÷21=9.14, not divisor; 196÷22=8.72, not divisor; 196÷23=...", truncated mid-loop.
- **dg η=0.5** (16384 tokens, wrong): correct prime factorization, computes "(2+1)(2+1) = 3×3 = 9" inside the trace, then loops on rechecking, never closes with `\boxed{}`. the answer was in the model's head, just never formatted.
- **dg η=2.0** (534 tokens, correct): textbook structure, factorize, apply formula, list divisors, `\boxed{9}`.

the original hypothesis ("stronger teacher → dg's surprisal mechanism activates more → bigger gains") didn't pan out. the actual mechanism we observe is that dg η=2.0 *abandons* imitation and finds a short answer-emitting policy reachable from the base model. og-on-r1d does roughly the same thing through a different path (is-corrected weighting). everything else collapses.

### what changes vs prior reports

three things shift materially under the calibrated comparator:

1. **dg-offline > bc on math is dead.** the previously-reported +1.6pp gap is now best-η dg (η=0.1 at 33.21%) tying bc-all (33.21%) exactly. the old gap was a comparator artifact. on math at 0.5b, dg ≈ bc ≈ untrained baseline.
2. **most distillation methods are tied with baseline.** bc, og, and 3 of 4 dg etas are within ±2pp of the 33.18% untrained baseline on greedy. the calibration didn't just shift numbers up; it tightened the whole cluster around the baseline.
3. **only rl methods exceed baseline meaningfully.** online grpo (+3.5pp) and two-stage (+4.4pp) are the only rows where the trained method is robustly better than doing nothing.
