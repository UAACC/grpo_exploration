## summary

eight experiments this week, all on MATH-500. split by student model.

**instruct student (Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct)**

- dg-s-grpo (5071171): eta=0.5, signed reward (−1/+1), Dr.GRPO loss. 31.01 ± 0.95%.
- dr.mixture-grpo (5070027): online student baseline (K_s=5 live samples) instead of fixed offline group mean. pending.

**base student (Qwen2.5-0.5B)**
- dg-offline (5077220): signed reward (−1/+1), dr_grpo, eta=1.0. 31.09 ± 0.97%.
- 0/2 reward + dr_grpo (5076961): binary reward scaled to {0, 2}, Dr.GRPO loss. pending.

- bc-correct-only (5077587): cross-entropy on correct teacher completions only. 28.63 ± 0.92%.

all completed instruct-student runs sit 1-2pp below the 33.18% untrained baseline. base-student RL (31.09%) beats both BC variants (29.79%, 28.63%); correct-only BC is worse than BC-all, matching the Instruct-era MATH ordering.

### A1. awr lr sweep

**method**: advantage-weighted regression. loss = `-exp(A/η) · log π_student`, where A is group-normalized advantage and η=1.0. three lrs spanning a 20× range around the script default (6e-7 in `AWR-offline/run_math.sh`):

| job | lr | greedy ± std |
|---|---|---|
| 5069827 | 3e-7 | 30.69 ± 1.01% |
| **5068963** | **3e-6** | **31.63 ± 0.82%** |
| 5072214 | 6e-6 | 30.74 ± 0.76% |

best at 3e-6 (5× script default). response surface is non-monotone — 6e-6 regresses relative to 3e-6, so the optimal is somewhere in between rather than "higher is better." all three sit 1-2pp below baseline.

### A2. rwr

**method**: reward-weighted regression. eta is deprecated (no exponential temperature); weighting is by raw reward r rather than group-normalized advantage. lr=3e-6 matches the awr-best lr for a direct comparison.

| job | lr | greedy ± std |
|---|---|---|
| 5072169 | 3e-6 | 31.73 ± 0.79% |

within noise of awr at the same lr (31.63 ± 0.82%). advantage normalization doesn't appear to help over raw reward weighting on this dataset.

### A3. dg-s-grpo

**method**: dg-offline (`DG-offline/run_math.sh`) with three changes from the standard dg sweep:

1. **signed reward**: r ∈ {−1, +1} for wrong/correct. shifts the zero point so wrong rollouts actively push down rather than contributing zero to the advantage.
2. **dr_grpo loss** (`--loss_type dr_grpo`): variance-reduced policy gradient objective.
3. **eta=0.5**: tighter sigmoid gate than the default 1.0.

| job | eta | lr | reward | loss | greedy ± std |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5071171 | 0.5 | 3e-6 | signed (−1/+1) | dr_grpo | 31.01 ± 0.95% |

sits in the same 31-32% band as AWR and RWR. the combination of signed reward, Dr.GRPO, and tighter gate (eta=0.5) doesn't move past the simpler methods at this lr.

### A4. dr.mixture-grpo

**method**: online + offline hybrid (`dr_mixture_grpo/run_dr_mixture.sh`). offline teacher rollouts supply training trajectories; the advantage baseline is computed live: at each step, K_s=5 fresh samples are drawn from the current student policy per question, scored with Math_Verifier, and their mean is used as r̄_student(qid). advantage = r_teacher_rollout − r̄_student. loss is Dr.GRPO.

this sits between pure offline GRPO (fixed group mean from rollout file) and pure online GRPO (both trajectories and baseline from student). the hypothesis is that a live baseline tracks the improving student better than the stale offline mean.

additional hyperparameters (differ from the shared table above):

| param | value |
|---|---|
| K_s | 5 |
| baseline_temperature | 0.7 |
| vllm_gpu_memory_utilization | 0.20 |
| loss_type | dr_grpo |
| per_device_train_batch_size | 1 |
| gradient_accumulation_steps | 8 |

note: effective batch size = 4×1×8 = 32, matching the 4×4×2 = 32 used in other instruct runs.

|---|---|

### B1. dg-offline (signed reward, dr_grpo, eta=1.0)

**method**: dg-offline with `TRAINING_REGIME=signed_reward` and `--loss_type dr_grpo` at eta=1.0. same reward and loss as A3 (DG-S-GRPO), with eta relaxed from 0.5 to 1.0 and student switched from Instruct to base. isolates the student model and eta jointly relative to A3.

| job | eta | lr | reward | loss | greedy ± std |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5077220 | 1.0 | 3e-6 | signed (−1/+1) | dr_grpo | 31.09 ± 0.97% |

within noise of A3 (31.01 ± 0.95%), despite both the student model change (base vs Instruct) and the gate relaxation (eta 0.5 → 1.0). neither factor appears to matter at this operating point.

### B2. 0/2 reward + dr_grpo

**method**: dg-offline with binary reward scaled to r ∈ {0, 2} (correct=2, wrong=0) and `--loss_type dr_grpo`.

comparison to B1: the only change is the reward shape. signed (−1/+1) vs 0/2 under group normalization: for mixed-reward groups both reward shapes produce the same group-normalized advantage, since the group mean absorbs any additive offset. they differ only for all-correct and all-wrong groups, where group std=0 and advantage=0 regardless of reward shape. in practice the difference is in how the reward interacts with any un-normalized paths in the Dr.GRPO objective.

|---|---|---|---|
| 5076961 | 0/2 | dr_grpo | pending |

### B4. bc-all

**method**: behavior cloning on all teacher completions (`bc/run_bc_math.sh`). loss = cross-entropy on completion tokens only (prompt masked out). no reward signal, no advantage, no gating — pure imitation of the teacher distribution regardless of correctness.

hyperparameters (differ from the shared RL table):

| max_length | 2304 (= 256 prompt + 2048 completion) |
| beta / num_generations | n/a (not RL) |
| warmup_ratio | not set (framework default) |
| save_steps | 200 |

| job | filter | greedy ± std |
|---|---|---|
| 5077583 | none (all completions) | 29.79 ± 1.03% |

### B5. bc-correct-only

**method**: same as B3 but with `--filter_correct_only`: trains only on teacher completions that were scored as correct. equivalent to bc-all with wrong completions masked out. prior Instruct-student results from the 2026-05-19 snapshot showed bc-correct-only (31.13 ± 0.34%) marginally below bc-all (33.21 ± 0.43%) on MATH, both below baseline — the opposite of the GSM8K pattern. these base-student runs test whether that ordering holds for the base model.

| job | filter | greedy ± std |
|---|---|---|
| 5077587 | correct only | 28.63 ± 0.92% |

bc-correct-only falls below bc-all by 1.16pp. this matches the Instruct-era ordering on MATH (bc-cc 31.13% < bc-all 33.21%) and runs counter to the intuition that filtering to correct-only should help. on MATH the incorrect completions appear to carry useful distributional signal even though they're wrong; removing them hurts. both BC variants land below the base-student RL run (B1 at ~31.1%). comparison to B3 (standard GRPO) pending.
